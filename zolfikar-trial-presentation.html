Getting to Know You

Let's talk about you

Before we dive in, I want to understand where you're coming from and where you want to go.

01

Why do you want to learn Arabic?

Travel, religion, culture, business, connecting with family or friends? Your "why" shapes everything we do.

02

Have you tried learning Arabic before?

Any apps, classes, or self-study? Even if it didn't work out, knowing what you've tried helps me teach better.

03

What's your end goal?

Basic conversation, reading the Quran, travel, or full fluency? Let's set a real target together.

04

How many hours per week can you study?

Consistency beats intensity. Even 30 minutes a day makes a huge difference — I'll build a plan around your schedule.

05

Do you prefer structure or conversation?

Some people love grammar rules and writing. Others want to speak from day one. Most enjoy a mix — what sounds like you?

06

What languages do you already speak?

Knowing your language background helps me draw useful comparisons and make Arabic click faster for you.
